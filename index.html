How it works

Four steps, one less thing to worry about

  1. 1

    Book online

    Choose a service, add your task checklist, pick a time slot and save a card. Tell us upfront what to do if an item is out of stock: call you, substitute, or skip.

  2. 2

    We plan the route

    Errands are grouped by neighborhood, so your time is spent on your tasks, not stuck in traffic.

  3. 3

    Errands get done

    Your runner works through your list, texts you on arrival, at checkout and on completion, and photographs every receipt before leaving the store.

  4. 4

    Simple billing

    Time is billed to the nearest 15 minutes. You get an itemized invoice with receipts attached, charged to your card on file.
